## 1. What these decisions serve

The portfolio serves one person’s job search: discovery supplies opportunities, job-hunter evaluates and selects them, Circus Tent or Ringmaster executes commissioned applications, and the evidence substrate preserves outcomes and learning. [extracted: current request, Purpose]

The relevant capability profile consists of eight reusable playbooks—not a preferred list of job titles: structural deconstruction and isomorphic mapping; human asset optimization; compliance-to-automation; high-stakes de-escalation and field triage; hardware/software integration; latency-aware cognitive offloading; public/community governance; and data-driven campaign engineering. [extracted: 6-get-a-job.xml.txt, capability_profile.txt §2]

A useful job evaluation therefore separates ability, hard blockers, tolerability, pay uncertainty, playbook matches, and the legibility gap between the candidate’s evidence and the employer’s vocabulary. [extracted: 6-get-a-job.xml.txt, job_search_queries.txt §4]

**Operating priority:** produce a real, tracked production application from day one, using manual work wherever infrastructure is unfinished; retain the handoff’s “top five this week” as an operating target rather than a schema constant or a reason to manufacture unsuitable applications. [inferred: current request, Purpose; 1-handoff-brief.txt Parts VII and IX]

A tracked unsuccessful or outcome-unknown attempt is useful evidence, but it is not counted as a verified application commitment; fixture completions, browser benchmarks, and completed engineering milestones are not substitutes for production applications. [inferred: 1-handoff-brief.txt Parts III, VI, and X; 3-hellscape-blueprint.md §§4.6 and 7]

## 2. Decision discipline

| Class | Meaning and change rule |
|---|---|
| **Invariant** | A property supported by a paid-for counterexample or a short derivation; changing its mechanism is permitted, but weakening the property requires evidence that answers its derivation rather than implementation convenience. [extracted: 1-handoff-brief.txt Parts III, VI, and XI] |
| **Provisional decision** | A current tradeoff with an explicit reason and flip condition; adopting one does not certify that its premises have been measured. [extracted: 1-handoff-brief.txt Parts VII and XI] |
| **Wire mechanism** | A concrete versioned representation chosen to enforce a property; a field name, hash framing, revision sequence, or archive encoding is not automatically a timeless architectural invariant. [inferred: 1-handoff-brief.txt Part VIII; contracts/00-evidence-kernel.yaml; contracts/04-owner-separated-write-domains.yaml] |
| **Evidence gap** | A missing observation or source artifact, with a named way to resolve it; it must not be replaced by a plausible constant, endpoint, capability, or success claim. [extracted: 0-unified-meta-prompt.md, Epistemic discipline; 5-browser-qualification.md, Evidence discipline] |

The current product names are **Circus Tent** for the Python/Camoufox implementation and **Ringmaster** for the Rust/BrowserOxide implementation; the handoff’s `circus-tent-cf` and `circus-tent-bo` are historical aliases, not a shared execution implementation. [extracted: current request, Products; 1-handoff-brief.txt Parts III and V]
