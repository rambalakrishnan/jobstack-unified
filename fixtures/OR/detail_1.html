Job Summary
Serenity Vapors is hiring a friendly, dependable, and motivated Retail Sales Associate to join our team at our Aloha location! We are a locally owned business established in 2014, focused on providing excellent customer service and a welcoming shopping experience.

Compensation:

Starting pay: $16.80 per hour PLUS sales commission.
Typical commissions range from $500 - $1,000 per month, depending on individual sales performance.
Paid product training and opportunities for advancement.

Job Responsibilities:

Greet customers and provide friendly, knowledgeable service.
Help customers select products that meet their needs.
Learn about our products and stay informed about new arrivals.
Operate the POS system and accurately handle transactions.
Verify customer identification and follow all applicable age-restricted sales laws.
Maintain a clean, organized, and well-stocked store.
Restock merchandise, assist with inventory, and complete daily store tasks.
Work independently and as part of a team to achieve sales goals.

Qualifications:

Strong communication and customer service skills.
Reliable, punctual, and comfortable working independently.
Basic computer and cash-handling skills.
Retail or sales experience preferred but not required.
Willingness to learn about our products and follow company policies.
Flexible availability, including some evenings and weekends.

We provide training and are looking for someone interested in becoming a valuable, long-term member of our team. If you're outgoing, motivated, and enjoy helping customers, we'd love to hear from you!
