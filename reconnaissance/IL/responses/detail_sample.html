Bank Teller - Paris

at First Neighbor Bank, N.A. in Paris, Illinois, United States

Job Description

First Neighbor Bank is an employee-owned, community-oriented company that offers a fun, family-focused atmosphere, great company culture and an excellent benefits package! Are you looking to embark on a rewarding career in banking? Join our team at First Neighbor Bank as a Bank Teller! As a Teller, you will have the opportunity to interact with customers daily, providing top-notch service and building strong relationships within the community. This role is perfect for individuals who thrive in a fast-paced environment and enjoy helping others with their financial needs. You will be offered great benefits such as Medical, Dental, Vision, 401(k), Life Insurance, Competitive Salary, Paid Time Off, Employee Discounts, and an Employee Stock Ownership Plan. With a competitive pay rate of $16-$18 per hour, this position offers a fantastic opportunity to kickstart your career in banking. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Processes deposits, withdraws, and loan payments using teller proof capture systems; corrects misread items and balances/closes transaction batches; issues receipts after verification of amounts, signatures and/or balances. Balances currency and coins in cash drawer; buys and sells cash to vault to control volume. Promotes bank's e-services, such as online banking, mobile deposits and e-statements; sells bank products such as money orders and safety deposit boxes; makes referrals to First Neighbor’s investment centers and tax and accounting offices. Processes and balances electric utility payments, license sticker sales, coin counter, ATM cash and transactions, and credit card cash advances. Assists with customer inquiries concerning transactions and account information; will involve the CSR Supervisor if second-level support is needed. Refers customers to CSRs for new account opening or changes to existing accounts. Answers the phone and transfers calls appropriately; processes phone transactions.

Qualifications

Qualifications To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skills and /or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform essential functions. Education and/or Experience Associates Degree preferred; one year related experience and/or training; or equivalent combination of education and experience. Successful completion of First Neighbor on line training. Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities Demonstrates accuracy in the handling of cash, making financial transactions, and balancing of cash drawer. Highly proficient in practical math skills. Displays mental alertness, a professional appearance, punctuality, cooperativeness, and strong service minded attitude. Uses effective customer service and sales skills. Communicates clearly and concisely in ways that are easy to understand, verbally and in writing. Makes quick smooth transitions from one task to another. Adept in operating computers and proficient in software applications for Microsoft Windows, Outlook, Excel, Word, Power Point, internet and related computer platforms. Able to operate First Neighbor business equipment including but not limited to calculators, copying equipment, telephone systems, checking imaging technology, document scanners, shredders, coin machines, and cash counters.
