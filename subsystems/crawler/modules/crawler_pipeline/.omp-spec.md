## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[EK-ATOMICITY]**: A locally authoritative operation commits its admitted input, idempotency record, authoritative effects, journal events, and replayable response as one owner-local durable effect before acknowledgement. Install referenced artifact bytes durably before that commit. Cross-owner publication remains asynchronous; this rule does not authorize a shared transaction across owners.
- **[EK-LEASE]**: Claim atomically checks eligible resource state and absence of an unexpired lease, assigns a unique fencing lease_id and owner epoch, advances the entity revision, clears last_failure_reason, and appends evidence without changing resource_state. Completion checks lease ID, unexpired authority-clock deadline, entity/input revisions, and applicable owner/session epochs. A rejected stale completion has no effect.
- **[OWD-LOCAL-COMMIT]**: Commit an owner's authorized effect, revision advance, and journal evidence atomically within that owner's durability boundary. Do not acknowledge before durability. No owner commit acquires a peer-owned write lock or waits for a peer transaction, acknowledgement, or large-payload serialization.
- **[OWD-REVISION]**: A domain revision is a complete immutable snapshot, not a patch. revision=previous_revision+1 at the owner. The first revision is 1. The key (entity, domain, revision) identifies exactly one content digest. Identical retries are idempotent; different bytes under that key are an integrity conflict. Stale expected revisions or lease epochs cannot commit.
- **[OWD-RENDER]**: A Pass acquisition must satisfy its declared required content fields. Required content is present, non-null, and correctly typed. A Pass with missing required extraction is rejected as incomplete_extraction, not materialized as a valid job or reclassified as failed rendering. A non-Pass acquisition has pipeline_state=render_failed and reasoned missingness for every content value.
- **[OWD-FETCH-NOOP]**: An absent root accepts an initial complete crawler domain without a predecessor archive. An existing identical fetch key makes the acquisition write a no-op. No archive is created for that no-op. Independent discovery, lease, classification, and semantic transitions may advance the crawler domain while preserving acquisition and replacement_lineage exactly.
- **[OWD-REPLACE]**: A changed fetch key requires a complete archive of the prior logical row at the named replacement seal. That cut uses the current old crawler revision and the latest fully published downstream revision in the named ingest-side view. An unpublished downstream revision is excluded. The owner-local replacement checks both the old crawler revision/key and the selected local publication pointer before committing.

### 16.5 Atomic stage effects

- **Extract:** Listings, observation packet, exact occurrences, coverage delta, certified cursor boundary, and audit.
- **Snippet classification:** Genuine result/application or bypass evidence, listing transition, lease release.
- **Dispatch:** Work materialization, listing delegation, lineage, lease release, audit.
- **Fetch:** Detail materialization/reuse, work completion/failure, lineage, membership if eligible, audit.
- **Description classification:** Genuine/application or bypass, detail/listing transition, semantic evidence, membership, lease release.
- **Finalize:** Four-field synthesis, identity protocol, detail/listing completion, membership, audit.
- **Failure/reclamation:** Fence/clear, once-only retry, pending/terminal route, terminal dead letter, audit.

No database transaction spans network, model inference, signal emission, or human waiting.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/crawler-seam-mapping.md#20.2`

### 20.2 Same-key rule

Absent root accepts initial validated acquisition without predecessor archive.

Existing same key causes acquisition no-op and creates no replacement archive. Acquisition and replacement lineage remain unchanged. Independent discovery, lease, retry, classification, policy application, pipeline, semantic, and coverage-reference transitions may commit.

Changed response bytes under the same key are new observation evidence, not replacement content. A failed-render repair under the same key requires a future versioned freshness contract.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/crawler-seam-mapping.md#20.3`

### 20.3 Changed-key atomic protocol

1. Establish old crawler revision, digest, and key.
2. Select latest fully published downstream revision in the named ingest-side view.
3. Journal a replacement seal selection pinning both.
4. Verify complete locally durable registration, domain documents, and dependencies.
5. Stage archive extent metadata outside the critical section.
6. Stage new crawler revision with backward replacement link.
7. At commit, conditionally check both old crawler revision/key and selected local downstream pointer.
8. Atomically commit replacement, operational effects, journal effect, backward link, and durable retention protection.
9. Acknowledge only after durability.
10. Publish to read views asynchronously.

Changed checked pointers force rejection/reselection. A staged orphan is not a committed archive. No peer transaction, acknowledgement, or large serialization enters the ingest critical path.
