### Invariants
- **[LIFE-ORIGIN]**: An application fact must identify the commissioned origin, candidate, job, and attempt, and cite verified source and correlation evidence under the pinned origin policy. A local fixture fact cites its transaction; a production fact cites an actual verified origin receipt. HTTP 200, a submit click, a subject result, or an operator assertion alone is insufficient.
- **[LIFE-SOURCE-CHECKS]**: Verify source identity and attempt correlation independently. Preserve trace mismatch separately from authentication. An authenticated but uncorrelated message remains evidence of receipt, not an accepted claim/fact for an arbitrary attempt. Strong alternate correlation may establish the attempt despite a trace defect, but must be explicit and evidenced.
- **[LIFE-LATE-EVIDENCE]**: Evidence received after observation closure is appended at the current journal head. Publish new owner revisions and a new report over new input pins; preserve every prior seal, publication, and diagnostic. A current snapshot may legitimately contain newer facts than its latest report used.
- **[LIFE-MANUAL]**: Manual mode requires no automation subject, native run ID, connector version, or native binding version. Its dispatch and execution slots stay at revision zero with not_applicable. The recorder owns observation and journals human actions with manual provenance.
- **[EK-PROVENANCE]**: The ingestion authority assigns producer identity, provenance, owner_domain, role, assertion_kind, event ID, sequence, receipt time, and hashes using authenticated producer registration and the manifest's event registry. Untrusted callers submit EventSubmission, never JournalEnvelope. Reject submitted server/provenance/authority fields rather than trusting them.
- **[EK-CLAIMS]**: Preserve claim source, semantic scope, receipt time, and knowledge boundary. None, protocol acknowledgement, HTTP success, and execution completion do not establish application commitment. Authentication and correlation are independent checks. A valid signature with a trace mismatch remains authenticated but not reliably correlated to the claimed attempt.
- **[EK-ATTEMPT-SCOPE]**: An application attempt has immutable kind at commissioning. A laboratory fixture is practice and held_in. An employer origin is held_out; production requires an employer origin. derived_from references only the same kind. rehearsal_of links practice to production; performance_of links production to practice. These are lineage links, never evidence promotion.
- **[OWD-ATTEMPT]**: Attempt slots contain the exact lifecycle AxisRevision publications. Their outer slot revision equals the embedded axis revision; the axis, attempt identity, commission digest, owner_domain, and writer_id match registration and commission. The reconciler writes only report.

## 14. Origin verification and reconciliation ingress

A confirmation page, email, screenshot, portal response, or receipt enters first as a restricted observation artifact.

The fact recorder checks independently:

1. Source identity.
2. Receipt semantics under the pinned policy.
3. Candidate, job, origin, and attempt correlation.
4. Trace correlation or evidenced alternate matching.
5. Commitment versus as-of absence versus final absence.
6. Temporal or origin-sequence scope.
7. Practice, production, synthetic, and historical evidence scope.
8. Raw artifact integrity and availability.

HTTP 200, successful mailbox access, authenticated connector transport, a submit click, or a human note is insufficient alone.

A production origin need not implement the portfolio protocol. Its designated recorder may normalize verified receipts without becoming the origin.

If checks fail or remain unknown:

- Retain the observation and verification outcome.
- Publish no accepted `OriginFact`.
- Preserve application knowledge as unknown.
- Record an untrusted-observation or insufficient-evidence issue where relevant.
- Do not manufacture origin IDs, revisions, or application IDs.

Reconciliation reads exact source pins. It does not make live network lookups or query mutable rows as unrecorded evidence. Additional verification observations must first be journaled and then included in a new pinned revision.

An origin fact conflict is not resolved by deleting one fact or automatically accusing the executor. Multiple commitments, contradictory final facts, and mapping errors remain explicit issues.

### 11.5 Application facts

`ApplicationValue` contains cumulative `facts`.

Each fact contains:

`fact_id`, `origin_id`, `candidate_id`, `job_id`, `state`, `coverage`, `boundary_ref`, `origin_application_id`, `origin_revision`, `receipt`, `verification_kind`, `source_checks`, `authority_seal_ref`, `occurred_at`, `received_at`, and `evidence_refs`.

The containing publication binds the fact to the exact attempt.

- State: `committed` or `not_committed`.
- Coverage: `at_boundary` or `final_for_attempt`.
- Verification: `local_origin_transaction` for fixture truth or `verified_origin_receipt` for production.
- Final noncommitment requires origin-owned finality evidence.
- No accepted facts means unknown, not noncommitment.
- As-of absence and later commitment may coexist.
- Withdrawal, rejection, or closure does not erase historical commitment.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/job-hunter-seam-mapping.md#11.6`

### 11.6 Source checks

`SourceChecks` contains `authentication`, `correlation`, `trace_correlation`, `verification_policy`, `matched_by`, and `evidence_refs`.

Authentication is `verified`, `failed`, or `unknown`. Correlation is `matched`, `mismatched`, or `unknown`. Trace correlation is `matched`, `mismatched`, `not_observed`, or `not_applicable`.

Accepted claims/facts require verified authentication, matched correlation, nonempty matching inputs, and actual verification evidence. A sender’s serialized “verified” label is insufficient.

A trace mismatch may coexist with independently established alternate correlation, but that basis must be explicit.
