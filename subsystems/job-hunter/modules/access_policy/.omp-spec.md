## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[EK-PROVENANCE]**: The ingestion authority assigns producer identity, provenance, owner_domain, role, assertion_kind, event ID, sequence, receipt time, and hashes using authenticated producer registration and the manifest's event registry. Untrusted callers submit EventSubmission, never JournalEnvelope. Reject submitted server/provenance/authority fields rather than trusting them.
- **[EXT-AUTHORITY]**: Derive caller identity and grants from authenticated channel registration. Authorize verb, connector, subject, task scope, account/runtime access, artifacts, and control target. IDs and digests are not bearer authority. Application credentials do not grant connector control access.
- **[LIFE-AXES]**: Exactly one authority role owns each of the five axes. Each applicable axis has exactly one bound publication writer. A writer may publish only its axis and must preserve the identity of its authoritative source. A report writer must not hold execution/application write authority. No whole-attempt status or multi-axis update command is defined here.
- **[LIFE-SOURCE-CHECKS]**: Verify source identity and attempt correlation independently. Preserve trace mismatch separately from authentication. An authenticated but uncorrelated message remains evidence of receipt, not an accepted claim/fact for an arbitrary attempt. Strong alternate correlation may establish the attempt despite a trace defect, but must be explicit and evidenced.
- **[OWD-OWNER]**: Every declared domain has one authenticated logical writer. Entity registration fixes its field set, authority source, writer, storage-domain identity, and publication policy. Temporary workers act through that writer's fenced transition gateway; they do not become additional owners. Each owner_domain is distinct within an entity.

## 18. Privacy, evidence scope, and practice isolation

1. Candidate source history, resumes, references, employer receipts, native bodies, emails, and session-bearing captures are restricted unless explicitly reviewed otherwise.
2. Models receive only data authorized for that provider and purpose.
3. Hash possession grants no artifact access.
4. Redaction produces a new artifact with derivation evidence.
5. Logs exclude secret values and unnecessary personal details.
6. Raw secrets remain behind runtime access bindings.
7. Historical imported material remains `historical_unscored`.
8. Fabricated test bodies remain synthetic.
9. Real manual interaction with a fixture is current-run practice evidence, not production.
10. Practice receipts cannot support production commitment.
11. Hellscape forms are not employer-representative merely because their text came from a real job.
12. Practice completion never triggers automatic production dispatch.
13. New executor versions do not inherit capability presence or absence.
14. Curriculum results may inform future reviewed decisions but cannot amend an active commission.
15. Diagnostic replay invokes no browser, subject, control action, email send, calendar write, or employer mutation.

A production diagnostic may be re-derived offline. That is not live reproduction. Live reproduction is a new attempt.
