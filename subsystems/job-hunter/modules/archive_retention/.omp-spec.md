## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[OWD-REPLACE]**: A changed fetch key requires a complete archive of the prior logical row at the named replacement seal. That cut uses the current old crawler revision and the latest fully published downstream revision in the named ingest-side view. An unpublished downstream revision is excluded. The owner-local replacement checks both the old crawler revision/key and the selected local publication pointer before committing.
- **[OWD-ARCHIVE]**: Archive payload_json is the entire previous LogicalRow, including its registration, all domain fields, reasoned nulls, publication receipts, and seal vector. It is not a changed-field diff, crawler-only row, hash list, or lookup of the latest downstream record.
- **[OWD-RETENTION]**: A live replacement pins its archive root and every transitive immutable dependency. A retained later archive containing that replacement's backward link also keeps the earlier archive alive. Publishing a newer job revision, deleting a cache entry, or passing a TTL cannot release those dependencies.
- **[OWD-SEAL]**: A seal names a state and pins all domain revisions selected in a named read view. Its journaled seal-selection event pins the domain document digests and publication receipts before the resulting LogicalRow is registered. The row's input prefix includes that selection event, not its own later artifact-registration event.
- **[OWD-LOCAL-COMMIT]**: Commit an owner's authorized effect, revision advance, and journal evidence atomically within that owner's durability boundary. Do not acknowledge before durability. No owner commit acquires a peer-owned write lock or waits for a peer transaction, acknowledgement, or large-payload serialization.
- **[EK-ARTIFACTS]**: Citable artifacts are immutable, content-addressed, access-classified, and registered by journal events. Retain bytes while a retained manifest, journal prefix, diagnostic, replay bundle, or archive depends on them. Do not substitute a locator or hash alone for promised archived content.
- **[EK-HASHES]**: Verify all content digests. At sequence 1, previous_event_hash is Missing(not_applicable). At sequence n>1 it equals event_hash at n-1. JournalPrefix.head_hash equals the event hash at last_sequence. Resolve an EvidenceRef by journal, sequence, event ID, hash, and JSON pointer; all must agree. A hash chain detects changed content but is not authentication.

### 15.3 Crawler replacement obligations observed by Job Hunter

Job Hunter never changes acquisition to force a refresh.

- Same fetch key means acquisition no-op, even if a newer response or extractor exists.
- Independent classification/lease/discovery changes may advance the crawler revision.
- Changed fetch key requires a complete prior-row archive.
- The replacement cut includes the old crawler revision and latest fully published downstream revision in the named ingest-side view.
- Unpublished product updates are excluded.
- A concurrent checked-pointer change requires reselection.
- Product publication must make complete immutable downstream bytes available before installation.
- Crawler replacement must not wait for peer transactions or serialize large downstream payloads under its writer lock.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/job-hunter-seam-mapping.md#15.4`

### 15.4 Archive and retention

`W.ArchiveRecord` contains `record_type`, `schema_version`, `root`, `storage_manifest`, and complete `payload_json`.

The root hashes the canonical storage manifest, not the expanded wrapper. The manifest contains `encoding`, `schema_version`, `entity`, `registration`, `domains`, and `seal_point`.

Expansion reconstructs the exact complete old logical row without consulting mutable peer state. Hash lists or crawler-only snapshots are insufficient.

`FetchKeyReplacement` retains `replacement_id`, entity and registration identity, old crawler pin/key, complete new crawler revision, archive root, archived vector, retention pin ID, commit reference, prefix, and evidence references.

`RetentionPinRevision` retains pin/owner/writer identity, revision pair, dependent, archive root, state, release reference, prefix, and support. State is active or released. Release requires evidenced retirement; transitive roots remain protected.

Loss is an integrity incident, not successful garbage collection.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/job-hunter-seam-mapping.md#19.5`

### 19.5 Concurrent crawler replacement and product update

1. Product owner durably commits a new downstream revision.
2. Publication may lag.
3. Crawler selects the latest fully installed downstream revision in its named view.
4. Changed fetch key archives that exact complete cut.
5. A pointer race rejects/reselects the replacement cut.
6. Product acknowledgement never waits for crawler transaction completion.
7. Archive reconstruction remains exact after subsequent publications.
