## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[EK-REPLAY]**: Exact fixture replay consumes recorded external inputs and lifecycle observations, recomputes derived outputs, and compares semantic events, final state, and the diagnostic. It must not feed recorded derived outputs back as authoritative inputs. Diagnostic-only replay may re-derive a production diagnostic, but cannot replay an employer mutation.
- **[EK-DIAGNOSTIC]**: A diagnostic is a deterministic function of its immutable manifest, named journal prefix, and pinned classifier bundle. The bundle includes requirement and optional fix registries. It uses no current wall time, unrecorded network lookups, or mutable application rows as additional evidence.
- **[EK-HASHES]**: Verify all content digests. At sequence 1, previous_event_hash is Missing(not_applicable). At sequence n>1 it equals event_hash at n-1. JournalPrefix.head_hash equals the event hash at last_sequence. Resolve an EvidenceRef by journal, sequence, event ID, hash, and JSON pointer; all must agree. A hash chain detects changed content but is not authentication.
- **[EK-ARTIFACTS]**: Citable artifacts are immutable, content-addressed, access-classified, and registered by journal events. Retain bytes while a retained manifest, journal prefix, diagnostic, replay bundle, or archive depends on them. Do not substitute a locator or hash alone for promised archived content.
- **[EXT-FAILURE]**: ConnectorError describes failure to carry out this connector operation, not a composite task outcome. An observed native failed/completed/stopped claim is normal evidence returned by Observe. Local prerequisite and translation errors are not fabricated subject failures.

## 18. Privacy, evidence scope, and practice isolation

1. Candidate source history, resumes, references, employer receipts, native bodies, emails, and session-bearing captures are restricted unless explicitly reviewed otherwise.
2. Models receive only data authorized for that provider and purpose.
3. Hash possession grants no artifact access.
4. Redaction produces a new artifact with derivation evidence.
5. Logs exclude secret values and unnecessary personal details.
6. Raw secrets remain behind runtime access bindings.
7. Historical imported material remains `historical_unscored`.
8. Fabricated test bodies remain synthetic.
9. Real manual interaction with a fixture is current-run practice evidence, not production.
10. Practice receipts cannot support production commitment.
11. Hellscape forms are not employer-representative merely because their text came from a real job.
12. Practice completion never triggers automatic production dispatch.
13. New executor versions do not inherit capability presence or absence.
14. Curriculum results may inform future reviewed decisions but cannot amend an active commission.
15. Diagnostic replay invokes no browser, subject, control action, email send, calendar write, or employer mutation.

A production diagnostic may be re-derived offline. That is not live reproduction. Live reproduction is a new attempt.
