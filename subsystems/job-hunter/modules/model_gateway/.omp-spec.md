### Invariants
- **[EK-JOURNAL]**: An acknowledged event is durably appended. Sequences are contiguous and strictly increasing within a journal, beginning at 1. The first event is manifest_admitted and pins the complete immutable manifest. All subsequent events name the same run, journal, and manifest digest. Events are never edited, deleted, resequenced, or backdated into an earlier prefix.
- **[EK-NULL]**: Every null is Missing(value=null, reason=<one of the six reasons>). Empty arrays mean an established empty collection, not unknown contents. Unknown semantic states remain explicit enums; false and zero are never missing-value sentinels. manual_override requires an authenticated journaled operator decision and preserves the superseded observation.
- **[EK-ARTIFACTS]**: Citable artifacts are immutable, content-addressed, access-classified, and registered by journal events. Retain bytes while a retained manifest, journal prefix, diagnostic, replay bundle, or archive depends on them. Do not substitute a locator or hash alone for promised archived content.
- **[EK-TIME]**: Every Measurement and TimePoint identifies its origin, clock, and boot. Register clocks in the manifest or a clock_registered event. Persist monotonic offsets relative to that boot. A clock_registered event may atomically introduce the clock used for its own receipt timestamp. Never subtract across clock IDs or boots. UTC is annotation only.
- **[EK-PROVENANCE]**: The ingestion authority assigns producer identity, provenance, owner_domain, role, assertion_kind, event ID, sequence, receipt time, and hashes using authenticated producer registration and the manifest's event registry. Untrusted callers submit EventSubmission, never JournalEnvelope. Reject submitted server/provenance/authority fields rather than trusting them.
- **[EK-ATOMICITY]**: A locally authoritative operation commits its admitted input, idempotency record, authoritative effects, journal events, and replayable response as one owner-local durable effect before acknowledgement. Install referenced artifact bytes durably before that commit. Cross-owner publication remains asynchronous; this rule does not authorize a shared transaction across owners.

### 17.3 Model execution and costs

Model preference from the inventory is retained as configurable policy: choose the cheapest suitable model, with the stated Muse Spark and DeepSeek preferences treated as historical configuration rather than guaranteed current availability.

Every model call retains:

- Call identity.
- Provider/model identity and available version evidence.
- Prompt/schema/policy pins.
- Input artifact identities.
- Raw response artifact.
- Structured validation outcome.
- Usage measurements.
- Pricing version or missingness.
- Cost derivation.
- Authorization and disclosure scope.

Token budgets are declarations. Usage is evidence. Reservations, estimates, reported usage, and invoiced charges remain distinct. Cost-limit changes do not silently alter a frozen commission.

## 18. Privacy, evidence scope, and practice isolation

1. Candidate source history, resumes, references, employer receipts, native bodies, emails, and session-bearing captures are restricted unless explicitly reviewed otherwise.
2. Models receive only data authorized for that provider and purpose.
3. Hash possession grants no artifact access.
4. Redaction produces a new artifact with derivation evidence.
5. Logs exclude secret values and unnecessary personal details.
6. Raw secrets remain behind runtime access bindings.
7. Historical imported material remains `historical_unscored`.
8. Fabricated test bodies remain synthetic.
9. Real manual interaction with a fixture is current-run practice evidence, not production.
10. Practice receipts cannot support production commitment.
11. Hellscape forms are not employer-representative merely because their text came from a real job.
12. Practice completion never triggers automatic production dispatch.
13. New executor versions do not inherit capability presence or absence.
14. Curriculum results may inform future reviewed decisions but cannot amend an active commission.
15. Diagnostic replay invokes no browser, subject, control action, email send, calendar write, or employer mutation.

A production diagnostic may be re-derived offline. That is not live reproduction. Live reproduction is a new attempt.
