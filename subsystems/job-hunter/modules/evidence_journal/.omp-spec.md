## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[EK-JOURNAL]**: An acknowledged event is durably appended. Sequences are contiguous and strictly increasing within a journal, beginning at 1. The first event is manifest_admitted and pins the complete immutable manifest. All subsequent events name the same run, journal, and manifest digest. Events are never edited, deleted, resequenced, or backdated into an earlier prefix.
- **[EK-ATOMICITY]**: A locally authoritative operation commits its admitted input, idempotency record, authoritative effects, journal events, and replayable response as one owner-local durable effect before acknowledgement. Install referenced artifact bytes durably before that commit. Cross-owner publication remains asynchronous; this rule does not authorize a shared transaction across owners.
- **[EK-HASHES]**: Verify all content digests. At sequence 1, previous_event_hash is Missing(not_applicable). At sequence n>1 it equals event_hash at n-1. JournalPrefix.head_hash equals the event hash at last_sequence. Resolve an EvidenceRef by journal, sequence, event ID, hash, and JSON pointer; all must agree. A hash chain detects changed content but is not authentication.
- **[EK-PROVENANCE]**: The ingestion authority assigns producer identity, provenance, owner_domain, role, assertion_kind, event ID, sequence, receipt time, and hashes using authenticated producer registration and the manifest's event registry. Untrusted callers submit EventSubmission, never JournalEnvelope. Reject submitted server/provenance/authority fields rather than trusting them.
- **[EK-OBSERVATION]**: Producer IDs identify one sequence lifetime. Deduplicate identical (producer_id, producer_sequence) deliveries without duplicating effects. Conflicting content under that key is an integrity incident, not last-write-wins. Journal gaps, overflow, loss, flush receipts, and producer closure. A closed HTTP connection is not producer completeness or execution termination.
- **[EK-NULL]**: Every null is Missing(value=null, reason=<one of the six reasons>). Empty arrays mean an established empty collection, not unknown contents. Unknown semantic states remain explicit enums; false and zero are never missing-value sentinels. manual_override requires an authenticated journaled operator decision and preserves the superseded observation.
- **[EK-TIME]**: Every Measurement and TimePoint identifies its origin, clock, and boot. Register clocks in the manifest or a clock_registered event. Persist monotonic offsets relative to that boot. A clock_registered event may atomically introduce the clock used for its own receipt timestamp. Never subtract across clock IDs or boots. UTC is annotation only.
- **[LIFE-EVIDENCE]**: The journal is the source of truth. Every publication cites its supporting prefix and source evidence; it is itself registered after its bytes exist. Do not put the publication's own registration event or digest inside its derivation inputs. Imported evidence must be registered locally with its original scope and trust, not promoted by the act of import.
- **[OWD-LOCAL-COMMIT]**: Commit an owner's authorized effect, revision advance, and journal evidence atomically within that owner's durability boundary. Do not acknowledge before durability. No owner commit acquires a peer-owned write lock or waits for a peer transaction, acknowledgement, or large-payload serialization.

### 3.5 Journal and producer rules

Untrusted producers submit `K.EventSubmission`, whose fields are:

`producer_sequence`, `event_kind`, `event_schema_version`, `occurred_at`, and `payload`.

`K.EvidencePayload` contains `data`, `measurements`, `evidence_refs`, and `artifact_refs`. Every nested artifact identity is included in `artifact_refs`.

The ingestion authority assigns the journal envelope’s identity, sequence, authenticated producer, owner domain, role, assertion kind, provenance, recorded time, evidence scope, and hashes. Callers cannot submit those fields as trusted authority.

The first event is `manifest_admitted`. Sequences are contiguous. Identical producer-sequence retries deduplicate; conflicting content is an integrity incident. Gaps, loss, overflow, closure, and flush receipts remain explicit.

A local authoritative effect, idempotency record, revision advance, journal effect, and replayable response commit atomically within that owner. Remote employer effects cannot join that transaction.

### 3.2 TimePoint

Every `K.TimePoint` contains:

| Field | Type and rule |
|---|---|
| `clock_id` | Registered identifier |
| `boot_id` | Registered clock lifetime |
| `origin` | `engine_measured`, `browser_measured`, `subject_reported`, or `manual_reference` |
| `monotonic_offset_ms` | Nonnegative finite coordinate |
| `utc_annotation` | Date-time string or `Missing` |

Clocks are registered before use or atomically by an allowed clock-registration event. No subtraction crosses clocks, boots, or origins. UTC is annotation, not elapsed-time authority.

Employer dates, employment dates, email source dates, and future interview times retain their business semantics in source artifacts. They do not acquire invented monotonic coordinates. Their ingestion receives an actual recorder `TimePoint`.

### 10.1 Admission ordering

The commissioner resolves candidate, job, target, kind, task, execution, permissions, assistance, budgets, owners, and duplicate-risk review before dispatch.

The durable sequence is:

1. Install immutable input artifacts.
2. Admit a manifest with registered producers, clocks, and event registry.
3. Record/import authorization and selection evidence.
4. Construct and retain the commission referencing admission.
5. Register commission bytes.
6. Register the owner-separated attempt entity.
7. Independently publish the downstream attempt link.
8. Permit subject-mode Prepare and Start.

Registration follows artifact construction; records never cite their own future registration as input evidence.
