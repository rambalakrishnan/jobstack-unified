## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[EK-OBSERVATION]**: Producer IDs identify one sequence lifetime. Deduplicate identical (producer_id, producer_sequence) deliveries without duplicating effects. Conflicting content under that key is an integrity incident, not last-write-wins. Journal gaps, overflow, loss, flush receipts, and producer closure. A closed HTTP connection is not producer completeness or execution termination.
- **[EK-CLAIMS]**: Preserve claim source, semantic scope, receipt time, and knowledge boundary. None, protocol acknowledgement, HTTP success, and execution completion do not establish application commitment. Authentication and correlation are independent checks. A valid signature with a trace mismatch remains authenticated but not reliably correlated to the claimed attempt.
- **[EK-PROVENANCE]**: The ingestion authority assigns producer identity, provenance, owner_domain, role, assertion_kind, event ID, sequence, receipt time, and hashes using authenticated producer registration and the manifest's event registry. Untrusted callers submit EventSubmission, never JournalEnvelope. Reject submitted server/provenance/authority fields rather than trusting them.
- **[EK-ARTIFACTS]**: Citable artifacts are immutable, content-addressed, access-classified, and registered by journal events. Retain bytes while a retained manifest, journal prefix, diagnostic, replay bundle, or archive depends on them. Do not substitute a locator or hash alone for promised archived content.
- **[LIFE-SOURCE-CHECKS]**: Verify source identity and attempt correlation independently. Preserve trace mismatch separately from authentication. An authenticated but uncorrelated message remains evidence of receipt, not an accepted claim/fact for an arbitrary attempt. Strong alternate correlation may establish the attempt despite a trace defect, but must be explicit and evidenced.
- **[LIFE-ORIGIN]**: An application fact must identify the commissioned origin, candidate, job, and attempt, and cite verified source and correlation evidence under the pinned origin policy. A local fixture fact cites its transaction; a production fact cites an actual verified origin receipt. HTTP 200, a submit click, a subject result, or an operator assertion alone is insufficient.

### 17.1 Email intake

Gmail `Users.messages`, `Users.history`, and watch notifications remain adapter surfaces, not assumed receipt guarantees.

The adapter retains mailbox identity, message identity, raw bytes, provider metadata, ingestion evidence, and classification version. Notifications trigger retrieval; they are not themselves complete message evidence.

Duplicate notifications deduplicate by actual provider/message identity. History gaps cause explicit resynchronization evidence, not assumed completeness.

Interview invitations, rejection letters, acknowledgements, requests for information, and ambiguous messages may drive private work queues. Only independently verified and correlated receipt semantics may affect the application axis.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/job-hunter-seam-mapping.md#17.2`

### 17.2 Scheduling and outbound communication

Calendar events, ACLs, and free/busy are distinct provider operations. Job Hunter must preserve timezone, all-day versus timed semantics, provider revision, and actual response evidence.

Outbound email, notifications, calendar writes, and follow-ups require:

- Explicit operation authority.
- Pinned provider adapter semantics.
- Immutable intended payload.
- Stable local command identity.
- Durable intent before dispatch.
- Actual acknowledgement/effect evidence.
- Explicit uncertain delivery when needed.

These are private external operations, not fabricated job-application attempts and not read-only extraction.

No live send endpoint, callback verifier, or native idempotency guarantee is inferred from the endpoint inventory. Retrying an uncertain send requires the provider’s evidenced semantics or duplicate-risk review.
