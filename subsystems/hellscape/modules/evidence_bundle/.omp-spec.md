## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[EK-ARTIFACTS]**: Citable artifacts are immutable, content-addressed, access-classified, and registered by journal events. Retain bytes while a retained manifest, journal prefix, diagnostic, replay bundle, or archive depends on them. Do not substitute a locator or hash alone for promised archived content.
- **[EK-HASHES]**: Verify all content digests. At sequence 1, previous_event_hash is Missing(not_applicable). At sequence n>1 it equals event_hash at n-1. JournalPrefix.head_hash equals the event hash at last_sequence. Resolve an EvidenceRef by journal, sequence, event ID, hash, and JSON pointer; all must agree. A hash chain detects changed content but is not authentication.
- **[EK-REPLAY]**: Exact fixture replay consumes recorded external inputs and lifecycle observations, recomputes derived outputs, and compares semantic events, final state, and the diagnostic. It must not feed recorded derived outputs back as authoritative inputs. Diagnostic-only replay may re-derive a production diagnostic, but cannot replay an employer mutation.
- **[OWD-ARCHIVE]**: Archive payload_json is the entire previous LogicalRow, including its registration, all domain fields, reasoned nulls, publication receipts, and seal vector. It is not a changed-field diff, crawler-only row, hash list, or lookup of the latest downstream record.
- **[OWD-RETENTION]**: A live replacement pins its archive root and every transitive immutable dependency. A retained later archive containing that replacement's backward link also keeps the earlier archive alive. Publishing a newer job revision, deleting a cache entry, or passing a TTL cannot release those dependencies.
- **[LIFE-EVIDENCE]**: The journal is the source of truth. Every publication cites its supporting prefix and source evidence; it is itself registered after its bytes exist. Do not put the publication's own registration event or digest inside its derivation inputs. Imported evidence must be registered locally with its original scope and trust, not promoted by the act of import.

### 11.3 Hash reconciliation

EK journal prefix uses the last event hash, not the legacy hash of a canonical event array.

Semantic event/state projections remain separately versioned fixture profiles. Runtime alias substitution is permitted only in those explicit semantic projections, never silently in EK record hashing.

The full diagnostic is hashable without self-reference because replay status and bundle identity are separate records.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/hellscape-seam-mapping.md#11.4`

### 11.4 Export bundle

The M1 bundle retains the original human-readable layout:

- `manifest.json`
- `task.json`
- `build-receipt.json`
- `journal.jsonl`
- `authoritative-state.json`
- `timing-measurements.json`
- `manual-observations.json`
- `diagnostic.json`
- `diagnostic.md`
- `replay-receipt.json`
- `acceptance-checks.json`
- `artifacts/synthetic-document`

It additionally includes all transitive contract-required dependencies: owner manifests/prefixes, configuration, commission, registrations, owner publications, classifier/reducer/registry artifacts, import verification evidence, and artifact inventory.

Friendly filenames are bundle entries, not artifact identity. The bundle manifest binds names to exact digests and access classifications.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/hellscape-seam-mapping.md#11.5`

### 11.5 Archive representation

A retained complete session archive uses OWD’s:

- `ArchiveStorageManifest`: encoding, version, entity, registration artifact, all domain slots, seal.
- `ArchiveRecord`: record/version, root artifact, storage manifest, complete expanded `LogicalRow`.

The root hashes the storage manifest, not the expanded wrapper.

Expansion loads immutable local extents and reproduces the complete row, including Missing values and exact domain revisions. It does not query mutable peers.

Job fetch-key replacement is not a Hellscape operation. If imported job archives are retained as fixture inputs, their original completeness and retention semantics remain intact.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/hellscape-seam-mapping.md#11.6`

### 11.6 Retention

Retained manifests, prefixes, diagnostics, replay bundles, rows, and archives protect all transitive dependencies.

GC requires journaled proof that no retained root reaches the object and fencing against concurrent pin creation.

TTL expiry or a newer publication alone does not release dependencies.

Artifact loss records observation loss and preserves known identity. It is not successful GC.

Credentials remain outside ordinary evidence payloads. Restricted native bodies and sensitive receipts remain access-controlled. Redaction creates a new artifact and cannot silently preserve an exact-replay claim requiring removed bytes.
