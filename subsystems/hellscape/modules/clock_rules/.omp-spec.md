## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[EK-TIME]**: Every Measurement and TimePoint identifies its origin, clock, and boot. Register clocks in the manifest or a clock_registered event. Persist monotonic offsets relative to that boot. A clock_registered event may atomically introduce the clock used for its own receipt timestamp. Never subtract across clock IDs or boots. UTC is annotation only.
- **[EK-TIMING-INTERPRETATION]**: Exact describes the recorded value at its declared origin, not its truth across origins. Preserve subject reports unchanged. Assign an opaque report clock when native clock coordinates are unavailable; do not invent native endpoints. Compare independently derived durations only for matching semantic boundaries or an evidenced bound. A comparability_group name alone is insufficient. Never divide aggregate runtime into invented step durations.
- **[LIFE-TIME]**: All measured durations use Measurement with clock_id, boot_id, origin, discriminated estimate, and censoring. Endpoint subtraction requires one registered clock/boot/origin and ordered endpoints; exact zero is valid. UTC annotations and receipt order cannot establish cross-clock occurrence order. Opaque origin revisions are not lexically ordered as timestamps.
- **[EXT-TIME-NULL]**: Every normalized null uses Missing with one of the six kernel reasons. Every measurement has clock_id, boot_id, origin, estimate, and censoring. Register clocks before use. Never subtract across clocks or boots; UTC is annotation only. Endpoint differences and bounds obey kernel arithmetic.
- **[OWD-TIME-PROVENANCE]**: Every measurement retains clock_id, boot_id, origin, censoring, and an exact, bounded, or unavailable estimate. No cross-clock or cross-restart subtraction is permitted. Publication delay cannot be computed by subtracting arbitrary source and destination UTC annotations. Provenance and writer authorization are assigned and checked at ingestion.

### 3.5 TimePoint and ClockDescriptor

Every `TimePoint` contains:

- `clock_id`
- `boot_id`
- `origin`
- `monotonic_offset_ms`
- `utc_annotation`

Origins are:

- `engine_measured`
- `browser_measured`
- `subject_reported`
- `manual_reference`

Offsets are finite and nonnegative. UTC is a date-time annotation or `Missing`, never a basis for cross-clock duration subtraction.

Every clock descriptor contains:

- `clock_id`
- `boot_id`
- `origin`
- `source_id`
- `basis: monotonic_offset | opaque_report`
- `unit: ms`

Engine restart and browser document replacement create new clock lifetimes.

A native duration without native clock coordinates uses an opaque-report clock. Native endpoints are not invented.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/hellscape-seam-mapping.md#3.6`

### 3.6 Measurement

Every `Measurement` contains:

| Field | Constraint |
|---|---|
| `measurement_id` | Unique within the diagnostic |
| `name` | Registered semantic measurement name |
| `scope` | `Scope` containing kind and ID |
| `quantity` | `duration` or `scalar` |
| `unit` | `ms` for duration |
| `origin` | One of the four origins |
| `clock_id`, `boot_id` | Registered source lifetime |
| `method` | `endpoint_difference`, `source_value`, `evidence_bound`, or `unavailable` |
| `start`, `end` | `TimePoint` or `Missing` |
| `estimate` | Exact, bounded, or unavailable alternative |
| `resolution` | Nonnegative number or `Missing` |
| `censoring` | `none`, `left`, `right`, `both`, or `unavailable` |
| `comparability_group` | Identifier or `Missing` |

Estimate alternatives:

- Exact: `kind=exact`, numeric `value`.
- Bounded: `kind=bounded`, `lower_bound` and `upper_bound`, each numeric or `Missing`, with at least one numeric.
- Unavailable: `kind=unavailable`, `value=Missing`.

Rules:

1. Duration values and bounds are nonnegative.
2. Exact endpoint subtraction requires one clock, boot, and origin.
3. End is not before start.
4. The value equals the endpoint difference within serialization resolution.
5. Both numeric bounds require lower ≤ upper.
6. Exact estimates have no censoring.
7. Bounded estimates use `evidence_bound`.
8. Unavailable estimates use unavailable method and censoring.
9. Reported zero remains exact reported zero.
10. A comparability-group label alone does not prove compatible boundaries.
11. Aggregate runtime is never divided into invented primitive durations.
12. Human pauses remain workflow elapsed time, not framework primitive time.

`MeasurementEvidence` pairs the complete measurement with nonempty supporting references.
