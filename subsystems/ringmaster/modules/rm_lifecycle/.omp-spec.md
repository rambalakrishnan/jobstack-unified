## Invariants & Test Assertions
### Invariants
- **[EK-CLAIMS]**: Preserve claim source, semantic scope, receipt time, and knowledge boundary. None, protocol acknowledgement, HTTP success, and execution completion do not establish application commitment. Authentication and correlation are independent checks. A valid signature with a trace mismatch remains authenticated but not reliably correlated to the claimed attempt.
- **[LIFE-AXES]**: Exactly one authority role owns each of the five axes. Each applicable axis has exactly one bound publication writer. A writer may publish only its axis and must preserve the identity of its authoritative source. A report writer must not hold execution/application write authority. No whole-attempt status or multi-axis update command is defined here.
- **[LIFE-PUBLICATION]**: Axis revisions are complete immutable publications. The key is (attempt_id, kind, axis, revision); a different value under the same key is an integrity conflict. Each next revision is previous_revision + 1. Only the owner-local revision check and journal commit are atomic. Publication to readers is asynchronous and cannot require another owner's transaction, acknowledgement, or payload serialization.
- **[LIFE-EVIDENCE]**: The journal is the source of truth. Every publication cites its supporting prefix and source evidence; it is itself registered after its bytes exist. Do not put the publication's own registration event or digest inside its derivation inputs. Imported evidence must be registered locally with its original scope and trust, not promoted by the act of import.
- **[LIFE-CLAIMS]**: Execution publications contain only faithfully mapped, source-attributed claims from the commissioned subject/version. Preserve raw responses, mapping versions, declared scope, original times when available, and receipt times. An absent state is Missing, not an invented subject claim of unknown. Explicit unknown remains a valid subject claim.
- **[LIFE-ORIGIN]**: An application fact must identify the commissioned origin, candidate, job, and attempt, and cite verified source and correlation evidence under the pinned origin policy. A local fixture fact cites its transaction; a production fact cites an actual verified origin receipt. HTTP 200, a submit click, a subject result, or an operator assertion alone is insufficient.
- **[LIFE-RECONCILE]**: The reconciler reads pinned execution claims and origin facts and writes only report. Compare propositions at their actual scope and knowledge boundary. A stopped claim alone does not assert that an already dispatched origin transaction cannot commit.
- **[LIFE-REPORT-COVERAGE]**: Every claim and application assertion in the pinned execution publication is accounted for by a comparison or an explicit scope/uncertainty limitation. Any proved contradiction makes report.state contradicted, even if other comparisons remain incomplete. With no contradiction, complete comparisons yield reconciled; unresolved comparisons yield Missing with reason. No applicable subject claims yield not_applicable coverage, not a fabricated successful comparison.
- **[LIFE-LATE-EVIDENCE]**: Evidence received after observation closure is appended at the current journal head. Publish new owner revisions and a new report over new input pins; preserve every prior seal, publication, and diagnostic. A current snapshot may legitimately contain newer facts than its latest report used.
- **[OWD-OWNER]**: Every declared domain has one authenticated logical writer. Entity registration fixes its field set, authority source, writer, storage-domain identity, and publication policy. Temporary workers act through that writer's fenced transition gateway; they do not become additional owners. Each owner_domain is distinct within an entity.
- **[OWD-REVISION]**: A domain revision is a complete immutable snapshot, not a patch. revision=previous_revision+1 at the owner. The first revision is 1. The key (entity, domain, revision) identifies exactly one content digest. Identical retries are idempotent; different bytes under that key are an integrity conflict. Stale expected revisions or lease epochs cannot commit.
- **[OWD-PUBLISH]**: Owner durability acknowledgement and installation into a read view are different events. Transfer, validate, and durably install all immutable components before atomically advancing the view's domain pointer. The pointer update is bounded metadata work. Publication cannot expose partially transferred bytes.
- **[OWD-VIEW]**: A LogicalRow contains every declared domain slot and an exact version vector. Each nonzero slot embeds the complete domain document whose artifact digest and installation receipt it names. A zero slot means no publication in this view, or an explicitly inapplicable manual axis. Readers obtain one complete immutable row root.
- **[OWD-SEAL]**: A seal names a state and pins all domain revisions selected in a named read view. Its journaled seal-selection event pins the domain document digests and publication receipts before the resulting LogicalRow is registered. The row's input prefix includes that selection event, not its own later artifact-registration event.
- **[OWD-ATTEMPT]**: Attempt slots contain the exact lifecycle AxisRevision publications. Their outer slot revision equals the embedded axis revision; the axis, attempt identity, commission digest, owner_domain, and writer_id match registration and commission. The reconciler writes only report.

### 16.2 AxisRevision and publication

AxisRevision contains:

`record_type: attempt_axis_revision`, lifecycle `schema_version`, `attempt`, `commission_digest`, `axis`, `owner_domain`, `writer_id`, `revision`, `previous_revision`, `evidence_prefix`, `evidence_refs`, `value`.

Each next revision equals previous plus one. Same key/different bytes is integrity conflict. Publication is complete, immutable, asynchronously visible, and registered after bytes exist.

LifecyclePublicationReference contains `schema_version`, `attempt`, `commission_digest`, `axis`, `revision`, `owner_domain`, `content`, `registration_ref`. Connector axis values are restricted to dispatch, observation, execution.

### 16.3 DispatchValue

Required fields:

`state`, `local_handle`, `native_run_id`, `request_digest`, `delivery_refs`, `acceptance_refs`, `recovery_authorization_refs`, `reason`.

| From | Allowed next states |
|---|---|
| Unpublished | not_started, prepared, rejected |
| not_started | prepared, rejected |
| prepared | dispatching, rejected |
| dispatching | accepted, rejected, uncertain |
| uncertain | uncertain, accepted, rejected |
| accepted | accepted |
| rejected | rejected |

Dispatching requires durable handle/request/intent before delivery. Accepted requires correlated native evidence, not Start acknowledgement. Rejected after possible delivery requires nonacceptance evidence. Lost response is uncertain.

Same-state revisions add evidence but never authorize a second execution. Authorized resume preserves logical accepted state and records additional delivery.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/ringmaster-seam-mapping.md#16.4`

### 16.4 ObservationValue

Required fields:

`state`, `window_id`, `opened_at`, `active_deadline`, `drain_started_at`, `drain_deadline`, `closed_at`, `close_reason`, `at_close`, `control_observations`, `late_evidence_refs`, `measurements`.

| From | Allowed next states |
|---|---|
| Unpublished | not_opened, open, closed |
| not_opened | open, closed |
| open | open, draining, closed |
| draining | draining, closed |
| closed | closed |

Open/draining require opened_at and active_deadline. Draining requires drain coordinates and close reason. Closed requires closed_at, reason, and CloseSnapshot. Nonclosed values have inapplicable closed_at/at_close.

Close reasons are normal_completion, operator_stop, local_deadline, translation_failure, launch_failure, subject_terminal, connection_lost, infrastructure_interruption.

ObservationPolicy contains active_limit, drain_limit, native_cancel_wait_limit, cleanup_wait_limit, fixture_close_action. Employer scope requires fixture_close_action=not_applicable.

CloseSnapshot contains `closure_id`, `boundary_ref`, `known_versions`, `execution_knowledge`, `execution_evidence_refs`, `application_knowledge`, `application_evidence_refs`, `limitations`.

Execution knowledge is running, terminal, unknown, or not_applicable. Application knowledge is committed, not_committed, or unknown. Only evidence available at closure supports it. Closed-to-closed revisions preserve original closure fields.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/ringmaster-seam-mapping.md#16.5`

### 16.5 Execution and application axes

ExecutionValue contains a nonempty cumulative `claims` array. Existing claim IDs/content never change.

ApplicationValue contains a nonempty cumulative `facts` array. Ringmaster does not author it.

OriginFact contains:

`fact_id`, `origin_id`, `candidate_id`, `job_id`, `state: not_committed | committed`, `coverage: at_boundary | final_for_attempt`, `boundary_ref`, `origin_application_id`, `origin_revision`, `receipt`, `verification_kind`, `source_checks`, `authority_seal_ref`, `occurred_at`, `received_at`, `evidence_refs`.

Production uses verified_origin_receipt, not local_origin_transaction. Final absence requires origin-owned finality evidence. Missing receipt is unknown, not noncommitment. Withdrawal or rejection does not erase historical commitment.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/ringmaster-seam-mapping.md#16.6`

### 16.6 ReportValue

Required fields:

`state`, `coverage`, `inputs`, `evaluator`, `comparisons`, `issues`, `limitations`, `diagnostic`.

State is reconciled, contradicted, or Missing. Coverage is complete, incomplete, or not_applicable.

ClaimComparison contains claim_id, assertion_id, claim_ref, fact_ids, fact_refs, result, temporal_relation, rationale, limitations. Result is consistent, contradicted, not_comparable, or indeterminate.

TemporalRelation contains relation, basis, evidence_refs. Known before/at/after requires same-clock, causal, or origin-sequence evidence; receipt ordering alone is insufficient.

ReconciliationIssue contains kind, detail, evidence_refs. Kinds are origin_fact_conflict, subject_claim_conflict, insufficient_evidence, incomparable_scope, untrusted_observation, mapping_error, multiple_origin_commitments.

Rules:

- Any proved contradiction makes state contradicted.
- Complete noncontradictory comparison yields reconciled.
- Unresolved comparison yields Missing with not_observed or observation_lost.
- No applicable subject claims yields not_applicable coverage and inapplicable state.
- Narrow lifecycle completion may be completely not_comparable without proving application success.
- Outcome_unknown is compatible with later commitment.
- Entire-attempt definite noncommitment is contradicted by verified commitment.
- Earlier-boundary claims require temporal evidence, not later receipt time alone.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/ringmaster-seam-mapping.md#16.7`

### 16.7 Pins and snapshots

DomainPin contains revision and record_digest; revision zero uses Missing digest.

ReportInputPins contain dispatch, observation, execution, application. CloseInputPins contain dispatch, execution, application, report.

VersionVector contains all five numeric revisions. SealPoint contains seal_id, name, versions, evidence_refs; names are commissioned, observation_closed, evidence_updated.

AttemptSnapshot contains record_type, schema_version, attempt, commission, commission_digest, input_prefix, five axis slots, seal_point.

UnpublishedAxis contains revision zero and Missing value. Manual dispatch/execution remain zero and inapplicable. Later evidence cannot erase an existing publication.

Snapshots are derived read compositions. Report freshness is checked against its input pins; newer facts may legitimately coexist with an older report.
