### 14.1 ObserveRequest and cursor

ObserveRequest contains `handle`, `after`, `through`, `max_items`, and `wait_limit`.

ObservationCursor contains `handle`, `journal_id`, `sequence`, and `event_hash`. Sequence zero uses inapplicable hash; positive positions verify against the journal.

`through=Inapplicable` requests a new live cutoff. A fixed prefix requires zero wait. Waiting is bounded by call and descriptor limits.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/ringmaster-seam-mapping.md#14.2`

### 14.2 ObservationPage

Required fields:

`kind: observation_page`, `handle`, `after`, `through`, `next`, `has_more`, `observation_close_ref`, `items`, `limitations`.

For each item, `after < item.sequence <= next <= through`. Next is the last scanned position, including nonprojection events. `has_more` is exactly whether next precedes the cutoff.

Empty at cutoff does not imply native termination or no future evidence. Unavailable cursor history is explicit.

ObservationItem contains `event_ref`, `recorded_at`, `provenance`, `payload`, `measurements`, and `artifact_refs`. Metadata matches the journal; event_ref points to the complete normalized event. Every nested artifact is included for retention.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/ringmaster-seam-mapping.md#14.3`
