### Invariants
- **[EK-ASSESSMENT]**: Capability, opportunity, and result are independent fields. satisfied or violated requires a valid exposed opportunity, applicable controls, and supporting evidence. native_absence requires a required, evidenced absent native capability in the exact scope, not mere failure to reach a step. Unexposed work is not_exercised; blocked or invalidly controlled work is not_exercised or indeterminate according to what was actually observed.
- **[EK-DIAGNOSTIC]**: A diagnostic is a deterministic function of its immutable manifest, named journal prefix, and pinned classifier bundle. The bundle includes requirement and optional fix registries. It uses no current wall time, unrecorded network lookups, or mutable application rows as additional evidence.
- **[EK-REPLAY]**: Exact fixture replay consumes recorded external inputs and lifecycle observations, recomputes derived outputs, and compares semantic events, final state, and the diagnostic. It must not feed recorded derived outputs back as authoritative inputs. Diagnostic-only replay may re-derive a production diagnostic, but cannot replay an employer mutation.
- **[EK-CAPABILITY]**: A descriptor applies to exactly one subject_id, subject_version, configured mode, and interface surface. Its catalog must enumerate every capability for that scope. Every new subject version starts unknown until its own evidence establishes another availability. Omission and predecessor descriptors do not imply inheritance, presence, or absence.
- **[EK-ARTIFACTS]**: Citable artifacts are immutable, content-addressed, access-classified, and registered by journal events. Retain bytes while a retained manifest, journal prefix, diagnostic, replay bundle, or archive depends on them. Do not substitute a locator or hash alone for promised archived content.
- **[EK-TIMING-INTERPRETATION]**: Exact describes the recorded value at its declared origin, not its truth across origins. Preserve subject reports unchanged. Assign an opaque report clock when native clock coordinates are unavailable; do not invent native endpoints. Compare independently derived durations only for matching semantic boundaries or an evidenced bound. A comparability_group name alone is insufficient. Never divide aggregate runtime into invented step durations.
- **[EXT-DECLARE-NOT-ASSESS]**: The connector declares capabilities, mappings, observations, and source claims. It does not emit qualification, requirement satisfaction, or an authoritative application-success flag. Consumer assessment remains separate from capability availability and opportunity.

### 19.2 Assessment distinctions

RequirementAssessment retains requirement_id, facet, contract_version, scope, capability_refs, opportunity, result, evidence_refs, contrary_evidence_refs, finding_refs, rationale.

Results are exactly satisfied, violated, native_absence, indeterminate, not_exercised.

Satisfied/violated require valid exposed opportunity and controls. Unexposed work is not_exercised. Unsupported mapping is not native_absence. Invalid isolation controls do not establish leakage or isolation.

StepDiagnostic retains step_id, contract_id, status, attempts, authoritative_postconditions, measurement_refs, finding_refs. Status is passed, recovered, failed, blocked, skipped, or unobserved. Recovered retains rejected/failed earlier attempts.

StepAttempt contains ordinal, disposition, evidence_refs, postconditions, measurement_refs. PredicateResult contains predicate_id, scope, truth, authority_domain, evidence_refs. Known business predicates require actual owning authority evidence.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/ringmaster-seam-mapping.md#19.4`

### 19.4 Completeness and analysis boundaries

ProducerCompleteness contains producer_id, state, observed_ranges, missing_ranges, final_sequence, evidence_refs. Complete requires evidenced closure and no unresolved gap. Zero final sequence means evidenced zero emitted events.

DiagnosticValidity contains scoped_checks, producer_completeness, infrastructure_incidents, limitations. A failed control invalidates only dependent conclusions.

ObservationSummary contains opened_ref, closed_ref, closure_reason, lifecycle_refs, containment_refs, late_evidence_refs.

ApplicationSummary contains goal, as_of_sequence, postconditions, commitment_refs, confirmation_refs. Confirmation rendering and commitment remain distinct.

Analysis uses no current wall time, unrecorded network lookup, or mutable application row. Corrections and late evidence create new immutable revisions.

#### Reference: `seam-mappings/ringmaster-seam-mapping.md#19.5`

### 19.5 ReplayReceipt

Required fields are:

record_type, schema_version, replay_id, mode, run_id, manifest_hash, source_prefix, source_diagnostic, input_bundle, classifier, reducer, replay_engine, comparisons, result, limitations, invoked_browser, invoked_subject, performed_external_writes.

The last three are false. Exact fixture replay requires semantic_events, final_state, and diagnostic comparisons. Diagnostic rederivation requires only diagnostic comparison and inapplicable reducer.

HashComparison contains output, expected, actual, result. Missing required comparison yields unavailable; any mismatch yields mismatched; matched requires all required comparisons available and equal.

Production replay never repeats an employer mutation. Live reproduction is a new attempt.
